📜 How to Play Ludo

🎲 6 to Unlock

Roll a 6 to bring a new token out of your home base onto the track.

⭐ Extra Turn

Rolling a 6, capturing an opponent's token, or reaching Home awards an extra dice roll.

💥 Capture

Landing on an opponent's token on normal tiles captures it and sends it back to their base!

🛡️ Safe Stars

Tokens on starred tiles ★ or start colored tiles are safe from capture.

🏆 Victory

The first player to navigate all 4 tokens around the board and into the center home triangle wins the match!
